Free, 90 seats

A day for people who want a website that stays where they put it. You leave with yours online, on a domain or a key you control.

What happens

Morning: get it up

Four short talks, twenty minutes each, then everyone opens a laptop. By lunch the room has a page each, published from a folder rather than a dashboard.

Afternoon: keep it up

Backups, domains, what happens when the host raises the price, and how to move a site in an afternoon. All of it feels dull right up to the morning it is the only thing that matters.

Evening: the fair

Tables around the room. Show what you made, swap links, argue about RSS. Pizza arrives at seven and the doors stay open until ten.

House rules

  1. Anyone can come. Beginners get the front row and the first questions.

  3. Photos only with a yes from everyone in the frame.
  4. If you fix something for someone, show them how you did it.
