octobriana

Comic panels, painted armour, and the odd photograph, posted to Nostr.

Three rooms for the same pictures: a canvas you drag around, a sparse hang you scroll, and a constellation that keeps its pictures dark until you touch one.

Nothing here is stored in this file. The pictures come back from the relays every time the page opens, and each signature is checked before anything goes on the wall.

Only picture posts hang here. A note carrying more than a line or two of writing stays on the relay where it was published.
